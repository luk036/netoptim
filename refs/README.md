## Notes

- **Preprints vs. published versions.** `karp1978characterization` is the Berkeley ERL M77/47
  tech report (same text, different pagination); `dasdan1998faster` is the UC Irvine tech report;
  `young1991faster` is the arXiv preprint.
- **Alternates, not substitutes.** The Ahuja–Magnanti–Orlin file is the 1988 MIT ORC working
  paper (206 pp), not the 1993 book. The Kourtev file is his 1999 PhD thesis, not the 2000 book.
  The Khachiyan file is the Russian original; the cited English translation is not open.
- **Slides.** `luk2026_lecture_GEV_slides.pdf` is the ASP-DAC 2025 presentation of the GEV paper,
  and `luk2026_lecture_useful_skew.pdf` is a 2023 deck. Neither is a paper.
- **Lecture chapters.** The site has no consolidated PDF. The two clock-skew chapters are served
  as markdown and are already in this directory: `luk2026_lecture_algo4dfm.md` and
  `luk2026_lecture_net_optim.md`. The site index is <https://luk036.github.io/algo4dfm/README.md>.
- **Legality.** Author-posted copies (Blaauw, Sapatnekar, Dasdan, Grötschel, Boyd), institutional
  repositories (CaltechTHESIS, MIT DSpace, eScholarship, Minds, UCLA CSL, ZIB) and CC-BY
  proceedings (LIPIcs) are the cleanest. The SIGDA/UCI compendium and the university course
  pages are publicly served but not formally licensed as OA; use them accordingly.
- **Re-running.** `python refs/fetch_refs.py` queries OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and arXiv;
  `python refs/apply_found.py <json>` downloads a supplied `[{key,url}]` list and applies the same
  first-page verification. `_manifest.json` and `_status.csv` are the machine-readable inventory.
